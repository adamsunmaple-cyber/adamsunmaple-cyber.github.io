The Birth of Rome

From Legendary Origins to the Conquest of Italy

How did a small settlement beside the Tiber grow into a power that would shape Western civilization and change the course of history?

The Birth of Rome begins with the legendary world of Aeneas, Romulus and Remus, and the founding of the city. It then follows Rome through the age of its seven kings, the overthrow of the monarchy, the creation of the Republic, the struggle between patricians and plebeians, and the wars that transformed Rome into the dominant power of the Italian peninsula.

Drawing on ancient sources and modern scholarship, the book presents the wars, reforms, beliefs, institutions, and political conflicts of early Rome in a clear narrative for general readers. At its heart is a larger question: why, among the many peoples and cities of ancient Italy, was it Rome that ultimately rose?

This is not only a history of Rome's beginnings, but a journey from myth into history, and from a small community into a republic.

About the Author

Adam Sun

HISTORY WRITER · SOFTWARE ENGINEER · Sunbot EDUCATION FOUNDER

Adam Sun is a history writer based in Canada. He graduated from the University of Alberta with a degree in Computer Science and a minor in History. Although his academic and professional work has focused primarily on technology, he has maintained a long-standing interest in ancient Greece, Rome, and the civilizations of the Mediterranean.

In 2019, he travelled with a University of Alberta professor through Sicily and Greece, visiting a number of ancient sites. The journey inspired him to begin organizing his research on early Roman history. What started as travel and study notes gradually developed into a narrative history for general readers, tracing Rome's path from legendary origins to the rise of the Republic.
