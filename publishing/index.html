English-Language Publishing

More than translating words.

A book written for one language often needs more than literal translation to work for readers in another.

Our English Edition service focuses on preserving the author's ideas and voice while creating clear, natural English suitable for publication.

The result is prepared as an English-language book rather than simply a translated manuscript.

English Edition may include

  • Translation and language adaptation
  • Removal of literal translation patterns
  • Terminology consistency
  • Editorial restructuring where appropriate
  • English title and subtitle development
  • Book description and author biography
  • International publishing metadata
  • Print and ebook publication
